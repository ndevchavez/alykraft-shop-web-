About the maker

Flowers that never wilt,
friends you can hug.

AlyKraft started in 2024 with one ball of yarn and a lot of love. Every bouquet, doll and keychain is crocheted by hand in Atimonan, Quezon — no two are ever exactly the same.

Want something special for a birthday, anniversary, graduation or just because? Tell me your idea, your favourite colours and who it's for — I'll work it out with you.
